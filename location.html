🚗 Getting to Hiriketiya Beach

From Colombo / BIA Airport: Southern Expressway (E01) to Beliatta/Dickwella interchange (approx 2.5 hours by taxi).

From Galle / Mirissa / Matara: Coastal Matara-Kataragama bus or 30-40 min tuk-tuk ride along the scenic southern coast road.

From Ella / Nuwara Eliya: Scenic drive down via Wellawaya, Thanamalwila, and Tangalle (approx 3.5-4 hours).

From Dikwella Town: Only 5 minutes by local tuk-tuk (approx LKR 400-500).
