Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before paddling out with us at Hiriketiya Beach.

🏄‍♂️ Is Hiri Surf School suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. We welcome complete beginners and provide patient, step-by-step instruction on beach theory, safety, and in-water popping up.

🎒 What should I bring to the lesson?

Bring swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, and drinking water. We will provide all necessary surf equipment for the lesson (surfboard, leash, and rashguard).

🌅 What time is best for surfing?

Morning sessions are often a great choice because conditions can be cleaner and the beach can be less crowded. Our instructors can recommend the best time according to current conditions.

🏊‍♂️ Do I need to know how to swim?

Please tell us about your swimming ability when booking. For beginners, we stay in waist-deep water where you can easily stand. Our instructors explain all safety rules before entering the water.

👥 Can couples or friends book together?

Yes. Semi-private lessons are a great option for two people who want to learn together with a dedicated instructor.

🎯 Can I book a private lesson?

Yes. Private lessons provide 1-on-1 dedicated attention from a senior instructor for maximum wave count and fast progress.

📱 How do I book a lesson?

Choose your preferred lesson, date, and time through the booking form below or contact us directly on WhatsApp (+94 76 932 7173).

🌊 What happens if the surf conditions are not suitable?

Safety comes first. Our instructors assess the conditions daily and will advise you about the safest option or reschedule your session.
