Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about surf lessons at Hiriketiya Beach, Sri Lanka.

🏄‍♂️ Is Hiri Surf School suitable for complete beginners?

Yes, absolutely! Hiriketiya Beach has a gentle sandy bottom with rolling white waves that make it Sri Lanka's top spot for beginner surf lessons. Our patient local coaches provide step-by-step instruction on beach theory, paddling, and popping up.

🎒 What should I bring to my surf lesson?

Just bring swimwear, a beach towel, and reef-safe sunscreen! We provide all equipment: beginner soft-top surfboards, safety leashes, and UV rashguards at no extra cost.

🌅 What is the best time of day for surf lessons in Hiriketiya?

Early morning (6:30 AM – 9:30 AM) and late afternoon (4:00 PM – 6:00 PM) are best. These times feature lighter offshore winds, cleaner peeling waves, and cooler tropical temperatures.

🏊‍♂️ Do I need to be a strong swimmer to take beginner surf lessons?

No advanced swimming skills are required for beginner lessons. We stay in waist-deep water where you can easily stand on the soft sandbar, and your instructor stays right beside you at all times.

👥 Can couples or friends take semi-private surf lessons together?

Yes! Semi-private lessons (6,000 LKR per person) are specially designed for couples or two friends who want to learn together with 1 dedicated private coach.

🎯 What are the benefits of 1-on-1 private surf coaching?

Private lessons (8,000 LKR) give you 100% undivided attention from a senior local coach. You get individualized technique correction, maximum wave count, and faster progression.

🧒 Do you offer kids and family surf lessons in Sri Lanka?

Yes! We love teaching kids (ages 5-12) and families. Kids lessons (6,000 LKR) are conducted in shallow waist-deep water with soft safety surfboards and dedicated 1-on-1 coach supervision.

📱 How do I book a surf lesson at Hiri Surf School?

Booking is quick and instant! Message us directly on WhatsApp at +94 76 932 7173 or submit the booking form on this website to confirm your session.
